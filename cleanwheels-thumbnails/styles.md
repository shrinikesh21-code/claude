# 5 Thumbnail Style Directions (v4, dark)

> **LOCKED: the dark "Blvck" theme is approved and applies to every style. Style 1 (Classic Split) is the default.**

v4 changes: **dark "Blvck" filter** look (low-key, crushed blacks, desaturated, grain, vignette), night-time premium home carport, black fades behind the header and logo, lime as the only colour. Still 9:16, header + logo only.

Attach `logo/cleanwheels-logo.png` (lime on transparent) every time. Optional: a photo of the finished car, and a reference photo of the parking setting.

| # | Name | Layout |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Classic Split (LOCKED) | Vertical split, dirty left, clean right |
| 2 | Diagonal | Angled split |
| 3 | Stacked | Before on top, after below, lime line between |
| 4 | Detailer at Work | Full-bleed after shot, van in view, small before inset |
| 5 | Minimal Black | Pure black studio, hero shot, two small chips |

## Header options
Default is two lines: "DETAILING" white over the car name lime. Others: "[CAR]" lime + "REBORN" white; "SHOWROOM SHINE" white + "[CAR]" lime; "[CAR]" lime + "DETAILED AT HOME" white. Two short lines max.

## Style 1: Classic Split (locked), example: Land Rover Defender Octa
```
Create an Instagram thumbnail image, vertical 9:16 (1080x1920).

Attached: (1) the Cleanwheels logo, (2) [OPTIONAL: a photo of the finished, detailed Land Rover Defender Octa], (3) [OPTIONAL: a reference photo of the parking setting].
If (2) is attached, use that exact car as the AFTER half, keeping its colour and body details, and place it in the setting. Recreate the SAME car, same angle, same lighting, but filthy, for the BEFORE half. If (2) is not attached, create both halves yourself. If (3) is attached, match its architecture and mood.

CAR: Land Rover Defender OCTA: boxy, upright 4x4 body, flat vertical front, short overhangs, round LED headlamps with ring daytime lights, wide muscular stance, chunky off-road tyres on large dark alloys, bold protective front bumper and skid plate. Colour: Carpathian Grey. No readable badges or lettering on the car.

LAYOUT: one perfect vertical split down the exact middle of the car area with a thin white divider line. The car sits in the central part of the frame (roughly 28-80% of the height), front three-quarter view.
LEFT (BEFORE): the Land Rover Defender Octa caked in road dust, dried mud splatter, water spots, dull faded paint, brown brake-dust wheels, grimy glass. Flat, lifeless, even darker.
RIGHT (AFTER): the same car, deep mirror-gloss paint with sharp specular highlights, glossy black tyres, clean wheels, spotless glass, rim light tracing every edge.

SETTING: the car is parked at a premium private home, inside a modern covered carport / entrance courtyard at night: warm timber slat ceiling with thin black light strips glowing softly, dark honed stone floor with a faint wet-look reflection, clipped topiary plants in dark planters as silhouettes, a tall timber front door softly lit. Calm, quiet, luxury architecture photography. Identical setting, camera angle and framing in both halves.

LOOK ("Blvck" dark filter): dark, moody, cinematic, low-key. Crushed deep blacks, high contrast, heavily desaturated colours with cool teal-black shadows, fine film grain, soft vignette. Single soft key light plus a thin rim light along the roofline and shoulders. The ONLY saturated colour in the frame is the lime (#C8FF00) header word and the logo.

HEADER (the only text besides the logo), top of the frame, centered, in the upper 8-22% of the height, clean bold geometric sans-serif (Poppins / Outfit Bold style):
- line 1, smaller, white, uppercase, wide letter-spacing: "DETAILING"
- line 2, very large and bold, lime green (#C8FF00), uppercase: "DEFENDER OCTA"
Spell both lines exactly. No other words anywhere.

LOGO: the attached Cleanwheels logo (lime-green stacked "Clean wheels." wordmark, transparent background). Place it exactly as provided, never redraw, retype or recolour it, bottom-center, about 30% of the image width, with clear space around it.

BACKGROUND TREATMENT: fade the top and bottom of the image smoothly into pure black (#000000) so the header and the logo are crisp and legible. Subtle, not flat bars.

Blur or blank the licence plate. Photorealistic, high-end automotive photography, not cartoonish. NO other text of any kind: no labels, no BEFORE/AFTER words, no captions, no URLs.
```
