# 5 Thumbnail Style Directions (v3)

v3 changes: new **lime + royal blue** brand look, new logo, and a **catchy header** (white "DETAILING" over the car name in lime). Still vertical **9:16**, car parked at a **premium private home**. The header and the logo are the only text.

Attach `logo/cleanwheels-logo.png` (lime on transparent) every time. Optional: a photo of the finished car, and a reference photo of the parking setting.

| # | Name | Layout |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Classic Split (LOCKED) | Vertical split, dirty left, clean right |
| 2 | Diagonal | Angled split |
| 3 | Stacked | Before on top, after below, lime line between |
| 4 | Detailer at Work | Full-bleed after shot, van in view, small before inset |
| 5 | Minimal Blue | Royal blue studio, hero shot, two small chips |

## Header options (the project rotates these, or you pick)
Default is two lines: "DETAILING" white, car name lime. Other formats the project can use:
- "[CAR]" lime + "REBORN" white
- "SHOWROOM SHINE" white + "[CAR]" lime
- "[CAR]" lime + "DETAILED AT HOME" white
Keep it to two short lines.

## Style 1: Classic Split (locked), example: Toyota Fortuner
```
Create an Instagram thumbnail image, vertical 9:16 (1080x1920).

Attached: (1) the Cleanwheels logo, (2) [OPTIONAL: a photo of the finished, detailed Toyota Fortuner], (3) [OPTIONAL: a reference photo of the parking setting].
If (2) is attached, use that exact car as the AFTER half, keeping its colour and body details, and place it in the setting. Recreate the SAME car, same angle, same lighting, but filthy, for the BEFORE half. If (2) is not attached, create both halves yourself. If (3) is attached, match its architecture and mood.

LAYOUT: one perfect vertical split down the exact middle of the car area with a thin white divider line. The car sits in the central part of the frame (roughly 28-80% of the height), front three-quarter view.
LEFT (BEFORE): white Toyota Fortuner covered in road dust, mud splatter, water spots, dull paint, brown brake-dust wheels, grimy glass. Slightly flatter, cooler light.
RIGHT (AFTER): the same Fortuner, mirror-gloss paint, sharp reflections, glossy black tyres, bright clean alloys, spotless glass. Slightly brighter, warmer light.

SETTING: the car is parked at a premium private home, inside a modern covered carport / entrance courtyard: warm timber slat ceiling with thin black light strips, honed stone floor, a few clipped topiary plants in dark planters, a tall timber front door, soft golden natural light filtering through greenery. Calm, quiet, luxury architecture photography. Identical setting, camera angle and framing in both halves.

HEADER (the only text besides the logo), top of the frame, centered, in the upper 8-22% of the height, clean bold geometric sans-serif (Poppins / Outfit Bold style):
- line 1, smaller, white, uppercase, wide letter-spacing: "DETAILING"
- line 2, very large and bold, lime green (#C8FF00), uppercase: "FORTUNER"
Spell both words exactly. No other words anywhere.

LOGO: the attached Cleanwheels logo (lime-green stacked "Clean wheels." wordmark, transparent background). Place it exactly as provided, never redraw, retype or recolour it, bottom-center, about 30% of the image width, with clear space around it.

BACKGROUND TREATMENT: blend the top and bottom of the photo into a deep royal blue (#0000A0) gradient so the header and the logo are crisp and legible. The blue should feel like the brand colour, not a flat bar.

Blur or blank the licence plate. Photorealistic, high-end automotive photography, not cartoonish. NO other text of any kind: no labels, no BEFORE/AFTER words, no captions, no URLs.
```
