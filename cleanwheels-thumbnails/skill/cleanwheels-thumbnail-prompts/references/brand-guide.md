# Cleanwheels Brand Guide (for thumbnails)

> **LOCKED: dark "Blvck" theme, Classic Split layout, 9:16, header + logo only.** Approved by the user. Do not drift from it.

**Business:** Mobile car detailing (the latest poster says Mysore, doorstep service). Our detailing van comes to the customer's home and we wash and detail the car in their driveway. That is the selling point, so thumbnails should hint at it ("We come to you").

## Logo
- File: `logo/cleanwheels-logo.png`: lime-green stacked "Clean wheels." wordmark on a transparent background (taken from the Mysore poster).
- Always attached to ChatGPT as a reference image. Never ask ChatGPT to redraw, retype or recolour it.
- Placement: bottom-center, about 30% of the image width, clear space around it.

## Colours (dark theme)
| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Base | Black `#000000`, deep shadows with a cool teal-black tint |
| Brand accent (logo, car name, highlights) | Lime `#C8FF00` |
| Text | White `#FFFFFF` |

Lime is the only saturated colour in the frame. Royal blue `#0000A0` stays a brand colour for posters but is not used on thumbnails now. Never use red, orange or gold.

## Typography
Bold rounded geometric sans (Poppins / Outfit Bold style, matching the poster). Header is uppercase, two lines: small white line over a big lime line (the car name).

## Photo look ("Blvck" dark filter)
- Dark, moody, cinematic, low-key. Crushed blacks, high contrast, desaturated colours, fine film grain, soft vignette, single key light plus thin rim light.
- Same car, same angle, same camera height and same lighting in both halves. Only the condition changes.
- BEFORE: dusty, water spots, road grime, dull paint, brown brake-dust wheels, dirty glass.
- AFTER: deep mirror-gloss paint, crisp reflections, glossy black tyres, clean bright wheels, spotless glass.
- Realistic, high-end automotive photography. Never cartoonish, never over-saturated.
- Licence plates blurred or blank.

## Text rules
- Only two pieces of text on the thumbnail: the **header** and the **logo**.
- Header: two short lines, default `DETAILING` (white, small) over `[CAR NAME]` (lime, huge). Exact spelling, uppercase.
- No BEFORE/AFTER labels, no taglines, no location, no prices, no URLs on the image.
- If ChatGPT adds extra text, tell it to remove it.

## Setting
The car is always parked at a **premium private home**: a modern covered carport or entrance courtyard with timber slat ceiling, stone or polished concrete floor, at night with softly glowing strip lights, topiary silhouettes and a lit designer front door. This shows we detail the car at the customer's own house. Same setting in both halves.

## Format
Instagram thumbnail / Reel cover, vertical **9:16, 1080 x 1920 px**. Keep the car and the split line inside the central safe area (about the middle 60% of the height) so the 3:4 and 1:1 grid crops still work. Header sits in the upper 8-22%, logo bottom-center above the bottom 8%. Both sit on a black gradient blended into the photo.
