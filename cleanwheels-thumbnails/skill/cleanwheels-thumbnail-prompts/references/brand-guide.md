# Cleanwheels Brand Guide (for thumbnails)

**Business:** Mobile car detailing in Adelaide. Our detailing van comes to the customer's home and we wash and detail the car in their driveway. That is the selling point, so thumbnails should hint at it ("We come to you").

## Logo
- File: `logo/cleanwheels-logo.png` (electric-blue "Cleanwheels" wordmark with a small circled mark top-right, on black).
- Always attached to ChatGPT as a reference image. Never ask ChatGPT to redraw or retype the logo.
- Placement: bottom-center or top-left, about 25-30% of the image width, with clear space around it.

## Colours
| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Background / base | Black `#000000` |
| Brand accent | Electric blue `#0A3CFF` (match the logo) |
| Text | White `#FFFFFF` |

Blue is used for the logo, split line, small accents and highlights. Never use red, green or gold.

## Typography
Bold, tight, clean grotesk in the style of Helvetica Neue Bold / Inter Bold. Uppercase for headlines, sentence case for small lines. White text, with one key word in electric blue if needed. No script fonts, no drop shadows heavier than a subtle one for legibility.

## Photo look
- Same car, same angle, same camera height and same lighting in both halves. Only the condition changes.
- BEFORE: dusty, water spots, road grime, dull paint, brown brake-dust wheels, dirty glass.
- AFTER: deep mirror-gloss paint, crisp reflections, glossy black tyres, clean bright wheels, spotless glass.
- Realistic, high-end automotive photography. Never cartoonish, never over-saturated.
- Licence plates blurred or blank.

## Text rules
- **No text on the thumbnail except the logo.** No headline, no BEFORE/AFTER tags, no taglines, no car model. The logo is the only lettering.
- If ChatGPT adds any extra text, tell it to remove it.

## Setting
The car is always parked at a **premium private home**: a modern covered carport or entrance courtyard with timber slat ceiling, stone or polished concrete floor, warm natural light, greenery and a designer front door. This shows we detail the car at the customer's own house. Same setting in both halves.

## Format
Instagram thumbnail / Reel cover, vertical **9:16, 1080 x 1920 px**. Keep the car and the split line inside the central safe area (about the middle 60% of the height) so the 3:4 and 1:1 grid crops still work. Logo sits bottom-center, above the bottom 12% of the frame.
