Paid powerplugs as cards

Your updated screen with the paid section rebuilt as a two-column card grid. Every card leads with its own icon art, so the grid reads like a gallery instead of a list. Tap a card for its detail sheet, scroll inside the phone, and switch the card style on the right.

Card style

What's in the grid

  • Eight cards, two per row, all inactive and priced per month.
  • Each card is tinted from its own icon, so colours never repeat.
  • Cards rise in one after another, lean toward your pointer, and lift on hover.
  • Tapping a card opens a detail sheet with the single-plug price and a link to MAX.

Plug names, one-liners and prices are placeholders. The eight prices add up to about ₹999, which is the crossed-out MAX price on your card. The saving label uses rupees to match the prices. Only four icon images exist, so the other four are hue-shifted copies.
