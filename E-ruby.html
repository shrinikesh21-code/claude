My story

The art kid who chose commerce

Growing up, art was my language. Calligraphy, doodles, clay, collage: I spent countless hours getting each one right. I started an Instagram page to share my work, and as it grew I hosted a giveaway and organised a small paint party. Maths was my favourite subject all the way through, and I finished Class 12 with 93.2%.

When it was time to choose, self-doubt won and I picked a B.Com over design. I don't regret it. I met incredible people and kept a 3.6 GPA. But every internship pulled me back to the thing I loved first. Now I want to bring both halves together, starting with a master's in Marketing & Creativity.

Research project · Consumer behaviour

Does a good story sell a good snack?

How does brand storytelling on social media shape Gen Z's trust in new D2C snack brands in India?

why this question

Gen Z follows founders before they buy products. I wanted to know whether a story can earn trust a label can't, and how new snack brands use it to break through.

how I approached it

A structured survey of Gen Z shoppers on what they notice, trust and share, rather than relying on secondary data or assumptions.

Key insight

People don't buy for one reason. Story, look and social proof pull on the decision as much as taste does.
